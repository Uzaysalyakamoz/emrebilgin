### 🔭 About Me

- 🎓 Pursuing an **M.Sc. in Space Science and Technology** at **Akdeniz University** (**GPA: 3.93 / 4.00**); earned my **B.Sc. in Astronomy and Space Science** from **Ankara University** (**GPA: 3.11 / 4.00**).
- 🌌 Working as a **Graduate Research Scholar** on a **TÜBİTAK 1001** funded project (*"Spectroscopic and Photometric Investigation of Galactic SNRs at Optical Wavelengths"*), handling optical data acquisition, flux calibration, and multi-wavelength data reduction at the **TÜBİTAK National Observatory (TUG)**.
- 💻 Developing spectral line analysis, electron density mapping, and scientific data reduction workflows in **Python** (`Astropy`, `PyNeb`, `Photutils`, `NumPy`, `SciPy`, `Matplotlib`, `Jupyter`).
- 🤖 Previously worked as an **Artificial Intelligence Trainer** at **TELUS Digital AI Data Solutions**, validating and optimizing AI models and ensuring data quality for scientific and technical applications.
- 🌠 Served 3+ years as an **Observer – Astronomer (Intern)** at **Ankara University Kreiken Observatory** managing night-sky telescope operations and FITS quality control, and taught observational astronomy & STEM workshops as a **Space Science Instructor** at **Bilim Başakşehir**.

### 📚 Publications & Conferences

- **First-Authored Manuscript (In Preparation):**

(2026).* **"Spectral Analysis of Supernova Remnant G108.2-0.6 with LAMOST"** *(Turkish Journal of Physics)* — Spectroscopic analysis of medium-resolution LAMOST DR11 spectra using `Astropy` and `PyNeb` to measure Hα, [N II], and [S II] line fluxes and derive electron density distribution maps.

  Delivered an oral presentation as first author at the **Turkish Physical Society 42nd International Physics Congress (TPS42)** titled **"Spectral Analysis of Supernova Remnant G108.2-0.6 with LAMOST"**.
